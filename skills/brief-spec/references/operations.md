# Operations profile

Use sections in this order: Event, Impact, Current state, Actions, Recovery, Follow-up.

Use timestamps and observable state. Distinguish service recovery from root-cause resolution and
from prevention. Never claim deployment, publication, or rollback without direct evidence.

In Impact, use numbers: who or what was affected, how much, and for how long. In Current state,
keep a short timeline of what happened and when, most recent first.
